<objective>
Build the content-addressed model cache that MCHE-02/MCHE-03 describe: `ElmModelCache` composes plan 02-01's verified-manifest front door and FetchFn seam into the full acquire pipeline — single-flight download, stage→verify→smoke→rename atomic publish, size-first hit path, pin-refcount RAII, `.bad-<hash>` quarantine, byte-capped LRU eviction, and directory-scan restart rebuild.
