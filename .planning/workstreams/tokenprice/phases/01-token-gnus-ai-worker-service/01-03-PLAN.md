  - Otherwise: if `Date.now() < this.holdOffUntil` skip joining a fetch (go straight to the stale/error path); if a batch is `inflight`, open/join a NEW collecting batch (bounded ≤2 transient overlap).   - Window driven by `setTimeout(flush, BATCH_WINDOW_MS)` — NOT alarms (too coarse for 15ms), NOT setInterval
