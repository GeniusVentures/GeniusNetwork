<objective>
Consumer callbacks are safe to call from: Bitswap never invokes them — nor the new synchronous
replay — under any internal mutex, a re-entrant consumer (cancel its own request, chain a new
one) cannot deadlock the protocol or trigger use-after-free, and a callback registered on an
already-terminal request receives the stored outcome immediately (D-01..D-03) with no fresh
timer ever armed (SC3 clause 2).

Three surgical seams on top of the Phase 1 shape (HandleResponse already dispatches after
mutex_ release): (1) AddCallback gains a completed-branch that replays the stored terminal
result synchronously after lock release and never arms the timer; (2) messageSent
find-or-replaces the requestContexts_ entry under mutexRequestCallbacks_ and calls AddCallback
strictly outside it — the single BSTP-04 restructure site (D-10 inventory site 3); (3)
processReceivedBlocks drops its erase so the completion flag selects the single winner and
completed entries persist until replaced (D-04). The race suite extends from 7 to 13 cases
(reentrancy both flavors, replay both outcomes, the D-08 SC3×BSTP-05 collision, the canceled
drop), and a WSL ASan run supplies supplementary UAF evidence (D-07).

Purpose: close the audit C-7 P0 ("callbacks reportedly invoked while holding mutex_") at the
block-request tier and deliver the TEST-01 full race-ordering regression suite as the
observable definition of closed.
Output: dev_timer_correctness (from d2ec18af, never pushed) with the three seams, a green
13-case deterministic Windows suite, a passing server-client regression, and a clean WSL ASan
run — all content-probeable for Phase 4's pin.
</objective>
