<objective>
Close BUILD-01 as a verify-and-close bug: prove (with a persistent regression test, not just a one-time manual repro) that `ProcessingManager::Create()`'s Vulkan capability probe no longer deadlocks, and document the evidence trail honestly.

Per 18-CONTEXT.md D-01/D-02, the described `VulkanInitMutex` self-deadlock was already fixed by commit `528a92a` in the `SGProcessingManager` submodule (predating the todo's 2026-08-10 filing) — this plan does not change any locking code. Output: One new `TEST_F` (`VulkanConcurrentInitTest.CreateSucceedsWithRealVulkanDevicePresent`), one new CMake `TIMEOUT` property on the `vulkan_init_concurrency_test` ctest target, and `18-BUILD-STABILITY-EVIDENCE.md` documenting the closure proof.
