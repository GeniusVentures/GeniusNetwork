<objective>
Implement a concrete, empirically-justified fix for the open VALD-01/SC1 gap on the MNN float32 fixture: widen `QuantizeFloatBuffer`'s fixed rounding grid from S=2^20 to S=2^14 (~64x wider), informed by Phase 13's own fresh post-quantization measurement showing the original ~9x margin was insufficient to prevent per-element grid-boundary tie-break divergence for this fixture's real data. Verify locally that this does not regress the existing canonicalization tests or SECV-01's wrong-result-still-diverges guarantee, then gather fresh real-quantization cross-machine evidence for the MNN fixture only (the render fixture already passes and is untouched by this fix).

This is an empirically-validated engineering mitigation, not a one-shot guaranteed solution: a fixed rounding grid cannot mathematically guarantee zero cross-hardware divergence for arbitrary per-element deltas that happen to land arbitrarily close to a rounding boundary. Widening the grid substantially reduces (does not eliminate) that probability for this fixture's actual values. Plan 13-05 diffs the fresh evidence this plan gathers and reports the honest outcome either way.

Purpose: close (or make measurable further progress on) VALD-01's remaining MNN gap, per 13-VERIFICATION.md's gap report and 13-SCOPE-BOUNDARY.md's own "Open Gap Against SC1" hypothesis.
Output: updated `quantization.hpp`/`quantization.cpp`/`quantization_test.cpp`, plus 2 fresh `xhw-mnn-float-refit_*.cap` files in Phase 13's `captures/` directory.
</objective>
