<objective>
Replace MNN's file-scoped `mnn_vulkan_mutex` (currently only guarding `MNN_Image`'s Vulkan session creation) with one shared, process-wide, header-declared synchronization primitive, and close the pre-existing gap where `MNN_String` and `MNN_Volume` call `createSession(MNN_FORWARD_VULKAN)` with zero synchronization at all today. This is D-04 (CTX-02) — the migration is required regardless of anything else in this phase; it fixes a real, pre-existing race condition in production code, not just prep work for `RenderProcessor`.

Purpose: Every Vulkan instance/device-creation call site in this process (3 existing MNN sites here, plus `RenderProcessor`'s new site in plan 01-04) must serialize through the *same* mutex object for CTX-02's coexistence-safety guarantee to hold. A second, uncoordinated lock defeats the purpose entirely.
Output: `include/processingbase/vulkan_init_guard.hpp` (new shared primitive), migrated `processing_processor_mnn_image.cpp`, newly-guarded `processing_processor_mnn_string.cpp` and `processing_processor_mnn_volume.cpp`.
</objective>
