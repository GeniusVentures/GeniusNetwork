## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-08 | Denial of Service | Malformed/adversarial Task.json_data() during tolerance-parameter resolution | medium | mitigate | try/catch around nlohmann::json::parse + sgns::from_json; any exception is logged and jobParameters silently resolves to nullptr (D-04's fixed-constant fallback) -- never propagates out of FinalizeQueueProcessing, never crashes the validating node. |
| T-15-09 | Denial of Service | The real fetchOutputData's FileManager::LoadASync call hanging or never completing on a malformed/unreachable outputUri | medium | accept | Mirrors ProcessingManager::GetSubCidForProc's own existing, already-shipped blocking-drain pattern verbatim -- this is pre-existing accepted risk in the codebase's established fetch idiom, not a new exposure introduced by this phase; no additional timeout mechanism is added here, consistent with the precedent this plan explicitly mirrors rather than invents. |
